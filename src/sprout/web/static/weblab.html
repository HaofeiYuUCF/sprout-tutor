Welcome to SPROUT

Learn the electronics behind smart farming by building a small greenhouse controller, one short project at a time. No experience needed.

  1. Wire it in an on-screen simulator. Nothing can break.
  2. Write a few lines of code, with hints when you are stuck.
  3. Build it on a real Arduino board, if you have a kit.

No kit yet? You can still do the simulator and code steps of every project. Use a laptop or Chromebook with Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.

Sign in

Type the sign-in code your instructor gave you. It looks like l_ab12.

The sign-in code works like a username: it saves your work and keeps it separate from everyone else's. We never ask for your name.

Choose a card

Each card is one small project with seven short steps: you wire it in the simulator, write the code, then build it on your own board. New here? Start with Card 1 and go in order, because each card builds on the one before. Your work is saved as you go.

New to the Arduino board?

Turn a 3D model of the board and click its parts to see what each one does. About three minutes, no kit needed.

First time with your board?

Check that your UNO works before you start a card. No parts needed.
