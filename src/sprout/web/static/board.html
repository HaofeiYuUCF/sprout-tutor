Four ideas to keep
  • Digital means on or off. A digital pin is at 5 volts or at 0 volts, nothing in between.
  • Analog means a range. An analog input reads how high a voltage is, as a number from 0 to 1023.
  • A circuit is a loop. Electricity leaves from 5V or from a pin, goes through your parts, and must return to GND.
  • Your code travels through the USB cable. The same cable powers the board and brings its messages back.
